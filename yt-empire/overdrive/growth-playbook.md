# Growth Playbook — first 100 subscribers

## Shorts strategy (the free traffic engine)
- Every long-form gets a next-day Short cut from its hook (pattern: `short-teaser.mp4`)
- Shorts formula: cold-open claim in text overlay ≤1s → 25–35s of the video's best metaphor → "full breakdown on the channel"
- 3 extra standalone Shorts/week from kinetic-card material (myth-a-day format: "KIDNEYS? ✗" style) — zero new generation cost, assemble from existing gfx
- Never link-spam Shorts descriptions; one pinned comment to the long-form

## Community tab (unlocks early; use from day 1 if available)
Three ready-to-post drafts:
1. **Poll:** "Be honest — what's actually stopping your progress? 🏋️ A) No time B) No plan C) Motivation dies week 2 D) I don't trust fitness advice anymore" *(D-voters are your core audience — reply to them)*
2. **Image post (use `thumbnails/_contact-sheet.png` cropped):** "33 videos are scripted and ranked by evidence. Which one should jump the queue? Top comment wins."
3. **Hot take:** "Unpopular opinion from an animated coach: your creatine isn't the problem, your consistency is. The most honest thing in fitness is a calendar, not a supplement shelf. Agree or fight me in the comments."

## Comment engagement plan
- First 48h per video: reply to 100% of comments (pinned comment promises it — keep the promise)
- Turn the 3 best questions per video into Short scripts (comment → content flywheel)
- Seed one question in each video's pinned comment (already written in posting packages)

## Thumbnail A/B (01A / 01B)
- Use YouTube's built-in Test & Compare on video #1 from day one: 01 (shock/"TAKE IT RIGHT") vs 01A (excited/"10 CENT SUPERPOWER") vs 01B (point/"MYTHS, BUSTED")
- Decision rule: ≥14 days AND ≥2,000 impressions per variant; adopt winner by watch-time share (not CTR alone); apply the winning emotion/word pattern to thumbnails 2–5 (rebuild = 1 command in `thumbnails/build-thumbs.py`)

## Milestone plan
- 0→10 subs: personal network + one honest Reddit post in r/fitness30plus weekly thread (no link-dropping; answer questions, channel in profile)
- 10→50: double Shorts cadence; community poll #2
- 50→100: collab comment strategy — leave the single best comment on every new Jeremy Ethier / RP upload within 30 min (algorithm-adjacent audiences)
