# Brand Kit — HonestReps

## Channel name
**HonestReps** — handle-style: `@HonestReps` (backups: @HonestRepsShow, @TheHonestReps)

Name options considered:
1. **HonestReps** ✅ — fuses the reference channel's honesty DNA with a lifting term; short, spellable, keyword-adjacent
2. RepLogic — clean but colder, no honesty cue
3. SwoleToons — fun but caps the audience at joke-channel
4. LiftTruth — on-message but clunky to say
5. CoachRep — good, kept as the MASCOT's name instead

## Mascot
**Rep** — a flat-2D vector "big brother coach": broad-shouldered, warm grin, coral tank top, navy shorts, orange wristbands. Friendly, never intimidating. He is the host of every video and appears in every thumbnail.

## Bio (2 lines)
> Animated, science-backed fitness — with the honesty the industry won't give you.
> Coach Rep breaks down training, nutrition and supplements so you stop wasting years (and money).

## Tagline
**"The truth about training — animated."**

## Colors (3 brand hex + white)
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (base) | `#1B2440` | backgrounds, outlines |
| Coral orange (energy) | `#FF6B35` | mascot kit, CTAs, punch words |
| Mint (proof) | `#2EE6A8` | science/positive highlights, accents |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | text, negative space |

## Typography
Display: **Anton** (bold condensed, all-caps punch words) — TTF at `channel-identity/fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf`. Body: system bold sans (Liberation Sans Bold).

## Visual motif
The **rep-counter ring** — a circular progress ring in coral→mint, used behind Rep's head in the pfp, in lower-thirds, and as the loading/transition wipe in videos.

## Rules
- Rep is IDENTICAL everywhere: same colors, same outfit, same line weight (master sheet = single source of truth)
- Thumbnails: navy background + Rep emotion + ≤3 Anton punch words + ONE focal object
- All on-screen typography rendered locally (never AI-generated text) → zero spelling defects
